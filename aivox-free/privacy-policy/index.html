Aivox Free Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Publisher: ABARBACH ILYAS. Privacy contact: contact@monkeycoding.com.

This policy describes Aivox Free for Android, package `com.monkeycoding.aivox.ai.auto.callrecorder`, and its current manual-recording functionality. It does not describe the separate Paid application, historical experimental applications or a future independently distributed Aivox Helper. Availability of a privacy policy does not imply Google Play approval or guaranteed recording compatibility.

1. Recording and microphone use

Manual Voice Recorder captures microphone audio after you grant microphone permission and choose Record. Choose Stop to end the recording. Audio can contain voices and other surrounding sounds, including sensitive information. The app processes audio to create a recording and allow you to play it back.

Automatic call recording is unavailable in the current Free candidate, including when a historical Auto preference is stored as ON. Android restrictions, device implementation and audio routing can make recordings unavailable, silent or limited to your own voice. No mode guarantees audible remote-party call audio. Before recording, obtain any required consent from everyone involved and follow the rules applicable to your use.

2. Information processed

The app saves recordings in app-specific storage on your device, using app-specific external storage when available and internal storage as a fallback. Its local library database stores file references, names or labels, recording times, duration, incoming/manual classification, categories and favorites. A label need not be an actual phone number. The current unavailable-Auto guard prevents telephony events from initiating automatic recording or processing an incoming-number field through that recording path.

Settings are stored separately and include onboarding status, recording-mode preferences, existing audio settings, optional cleanup settings and review-prompt status. Audibility-help answers and privacy-navigation pages are session-only; browsing them does not save a diagnosis or grant consent. Help can display Android version/API information to explain limitations; it is not a compatibility measurement.

The reviewed implementation has no app account, contact-book, location or app-owned remote recording-upload feature. This statement describes the current functionality, not the behavior of every system service on your device.

3. Permissions and user choice

Microphone permission enables user-started audio capture. You can decline it and continue using independent library features. Notifications communicate recording and saving status. The microphone foreground service supports an ongoing user-started recording and its Android notification. You can manage app permissions through Android's controls.

The corrected Free candidate does not declare phone-state, display-over-other-apps or battery-exemption permissions. Manual recording does not require those accesses. Optional call-state metadata is used only when legitimately available; unavailable or denied access does not by itself stop Manual recording. Without reliable evidence of an active call, the item is labeled Voice Memo, not inferred to be a call. This fallback is not an audibility or capture-success claim. Automatic recording remains unavailable.

Accessibility is sensitive access. The corrected Free candidate does not register an Accessibility service or request its activation. Historical research source is preserved, but is not an available Free feature. Any future independent Helper requires verified functionality, disclosures and applicable review; separate distribution does not establish approval for remote-call audio capture. The app does not claim that Accessibility has zero privacy or security risk.

Reading this policy, pressing Back or closing help does not grant a permission, choose a recording mode or start a recording. Grant permissions only when you understand and want the relevant function.

4. Backup, services and sharing

Android backup and device transfer are enabled in this candidate's configuration without app-specific exclusions. Depending on Android, the device and your backup settings, eligible recordings, app files, library metadata and preferences may be backed up or transferred. Do not assume that all app data stays only on this phone. System backup providers govern their copies and retention.

The current library has no app-provided upload or sharing action. No app-owned backend/upload endpoint was found in the reviewed source. The app does not pass recording audio to its Google Play review helper. However, it uses Google Play's in-app review service, which can communicate and handle review information through Google Play. Android and Google Play services operate under their own practices, including Google's Privacy Policy.

No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK appeared in the reviewed runtime dependency graph, and the current merged app manifests do not declare INTERNET or advertising-ID access. System services can communicate through their own processes; absence of those app permissions is not a claim that no third party handles any data. These findings will need review if dependencies or functionality change.

5. Retention and deletion

Recordings and library metadata are retained for use in your library until you delete an item or enabled cleanup attempts to remove it. You can rename, favorite, play or delete library items. Optional scheduled cleanup attempts to delete old non-favorites according to your settings; it is disabled unless enabled by the user.

Deleting a local item is not a secure-erasure guarantee and does not guarantee removal of earlier backups or copies outside the app. Manage Android backup and provider-held copies through the relevant device or provider controls. The app has no account feature, so no app-account-deletion workflow is offered.

6. Optional Aivox Helper

Manual recording works without a Helper. Aivox Helper is a future optional, independently distributed companion. No Helper download, installation, verified remote-party capture or approved activation tutorial is supplied by the current Free candidate. Any future companion would require its own verified functionality, disclosures and applicable compliance review.

7. Security limitations

App-specific storage and Android access controls restrict ordinary access by other apps. The app is not represented as providing additional recording encryption, secure deletion or protection from every device compromise. Device access, backups, system services and user-created copies affect confidentiality. Protect your device and avoid recording information you cannot lawfully or safely retain. No absolute-security or two-voice-recording guarantee is made.

8. Adults and children's privacy

Aivox Free is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not designed for or directed at children. This audience statement reflects the publisher's confirmed product decision; it does not assert that Google Play Console's target-age configuration has been verified. If you believe a child has used the app or you have a related privacy concern, contact the publisher without sending unnecessary sensitive recordings.

9. International use and privacy requests

Recording and privacy rules differ by location. Obtain required participant consent and comply with applicable rules. System and third-party processing may occur under those providers' own arrangements; this policy makes no country-specific storage or international-transfer guarantee.

For questions or to exercise applicable privacy rights, contact contact@monkeycoding.com. Requests will be considered according to applicable requirements and the app's actual data handling. Avoid including recordings or other sensitive information in an initial inquiry. A request to the publisher cannot by itself remove copies held by your device's backup provider or other third parties.

10. Policy updates

The publisher will update this policy when data handling, permissions, services or functionality materially change, identify the new effective date and provide any notice or consent required by applicable rules. Review the policy when using a changed version of the app.
